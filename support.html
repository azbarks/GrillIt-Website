Email support

For bugs, questions, or feature requests, send an email. Include your app version (Settings → About → Build) so I can reproduce the issue.

azbarks@gmail.com

For bug reports, include:

  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Steps to reproduce it (the simpler the better)
  • Your iOS version and GrillIt build number (Settings → About)
  • Whether iCloud sync is on or off

I typically respond within a few days — unless there's a brisket on. Thanks for your patience.
